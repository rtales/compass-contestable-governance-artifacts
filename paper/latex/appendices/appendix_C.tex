\section{Requirements for Prospective Early Warning}
\label{app:replication}
A prospective dropout-prevention task requires a risk-positive label (e.g., Fail/Withdrawn), an intervention horizon, features available before that horizon and aligned ranking. Temporal and student-aware splits, label audits and justified audit groups are also needed. The implemented OULAD success target and descending ranking evaluate a different task; their PosRate estimates cannot establish early-warning performance.

ASSISTments interaction correctness likewise differs from per-student support need. Aggregation, eligibility, treatment and actual benefit require separate evaluation. A changed target or feature set may require retraining; changed inputs require new inference. These operations exceed fixed-score replay.
